Place your gestures.csv file here before committing to the repository.

This file is the pre-collected training dataset that users can optionally
load on first launch of training_gui.py instead of collecting their own data.

After you have finished collecting and are happy with your data:
  1. Copy data/gestures.csv to data/pretrained/gestures.csv
  2. Commit data/pretrained/gestures.csv to your git repository

The main data/gestures.csv should remain in .gitignore since it is
generated during use and will differ between users.
